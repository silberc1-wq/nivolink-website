Services

What Nivolink does for your business

We set up and run automations that take repetitive front-office tasks off your team, so customers get fast answers and nothing falls through the cracks.

AI phone answering

An AI receptionist answers your business line around the clock. It answers common questions, takes messages and orders, and transfers urgent calls to your staff.

Phone & text ordering

Customers can call or text an order to the same number. The order is recorded, the customer gets an automatic confirmation, and your team sees it right away.

Customer text messaging

Two-way texting from your business number for order updates, appointment reminders, and replies to customer questions. Customers only receive texts after they opt in.

Automated reports

Record a session or a call, and Nivolink turns it into a written summary report and delivers it to the right people automatically, for example from a coach to the coaching program.

Opt-in updates & specials

Customers who sign up get your weekly specials and announcements by their choice of text, email or WhatsApp. Anyone can unsubscribe at any time.

Custom workflow automation

Have a task your staff repeats every day? We build automations around your process, such as intake forms, follow-ups, scheduling and data entry, and connect them to the tools you already use.

About Nivolink

Automation that works like a great secretary

Nivolink was founded to give small and mid-sized businesses the kind of always-on front office that used to require a full-time staff member. We combine AI voice agents, text messaging, email and workflow automation into one service. We set it up, run it and support it for you.

Our commitments

  • Each client's messages are sent from that client's own registered number, in that client's name.
  • Customers only receive texts after they give consent. Opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) work on every message.
  • We never sell or share phone numbers or text-message consent with third parties.
  • Human handoff is always available for anything the AI can't handle.
