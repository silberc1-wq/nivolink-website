Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FC PRO LLC, doing business as Nivolink ("Nivolink," "we," "us"), collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website, contact us, use our services, or receive calls, text messages, emails or WhatsApp messages from us or from a business that uses our platform.

Text messaging privacy: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all information-sharing described in this policy and will not be shared with any third parties.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, business name, email address, phone number, and anything you write in a form, call, text or email.
  • Consent records: whether you agreed to receive text messages, the date and time, and the web page or keyword where you opted in.
  • Communications: call recordings and transcripts, text and WhatsApp messages, and emails sent to or from numbers and addresses we operate, including orders placed by phone or text.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type and pages visited on our website.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide and support our services.
  • To process orders and send confirmations, updates, reminders and reports.
  • To send text messages, emails and WhatsApp messages you have agreed to receive.
  • To improve our services, keep them secure, and comply with law.

3. Businesses that use Nivolink

Nivolink provides automation software to other businesses ("Clients"), such as stores and coaching programs. When you call, text, order from or sign up for messages from one of our Clients, that Client controls your information and its own privacy policy applies. Nivolink processes that information only on the Client's behalf and on its instructions, to provide the service. We do not use our Clients' customers' information for our own marketing.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers that help us operate, such as telecommunications, messaging, email and cloud hosting providers, who may use it only to provide services to us.
  • With the Client business you are communicating with, for communications made through that Client's number or sign-up page.
  • When required by law, or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • In connection with a merger, sale or reorganization of our business, subject to this policy.

None of the above includes mobile phone numbers or text-message opt-in data and consent, which are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • WhatsApp: reply STOP or block the business number in WhatsApp.
  • You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us below.

6. Data retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Consent records are kept as proof of opt-in. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect information.

7. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
